# Deen — Privacy Policy

_Effective: 2 November 2026_

Deen ("the app") is an offline-first Islamic companion app for Android and
iOS published by Jamal Makame. This policy explains what the app does with
your information. The short version: **there is no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.**

## What stays on your device

Everything the app stores is kept only on your phone:

- your settings (language, theme, prayer calculation method, madhab,
  reminders, reciter and translation choices);
- your location as coordinates and a place name, used for prayer times and
  the Qibla direction;
- your Quran last-read position, bookmarks, Hifz progress and tasbeeh counts;
- Quran recitation audio and tafsir you download;
- error reports from the app crashing, which leave the phone only if you
  share them from Settings → Problem reports.

**Settings → Data & Privacy → Clear local data** deletes all of it, and
uninstalling the app removes it too. We have no servers that hold a copy.

## Location

When you allow it, the app reads your device's location to work out prayer
times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your phone. To show
a place name (and to find a city you type in), the app uses your phone's
built-in geocoding service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS), which receives
the coordinates or city name under that provider's privacy policy. You can
deny location access and choose a city by hand instead.

## Internet requests

The Quran text, translations, azkar and prayer calculations are bundled and
work offline. The app connects to the internet only when you ask for:

| Feature | Service contacted | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tafsir | api.quran.com | the surah and ayah number (or only the surah when you save it offline) |
| Ayah audio (Hifz) | cdn.islamic.network | the reciter and ayah number |
| Surah audio | server8.mp3quran.net | the surah number |

Like any web request, these services see your IP address. They do not receive
your location, settings or any identifier from the app.

## Notifications and sensors

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no push service is
used. The compass is read on the device for the Qibla screen and is never
stored or sent.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.
