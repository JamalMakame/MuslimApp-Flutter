إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ٤

“It is You we worship and You we ask for help.”

Sahih International
COMMENTARY auto_stories Full Exegesis

Linguistically, worship encompasses the utmost humility, submissiveness, and love. In the Shari'ah, it is an expression of complete obedience combining absolute reverence with sincere affection.

The shift in tone from the third person in previous verses to direct address (‘You we worship’) conveys profound intimacy and devotion. Serving Allah comes before asking for His assistance, indicating that obedience is the precondition for divine aid.
