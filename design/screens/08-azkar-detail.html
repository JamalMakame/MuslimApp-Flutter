"Aṣbaḥnā wa-aṣbaḥa al-mulku lillāh, wal-ḥamdu lillāh, lā ilāha illallāh waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamd, wa huwa 'alā kulli shay'in qadīr."

We have reached the morning and at this very time unto Allah belongs all sovereignty, and all praise is for Allah. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, without partner; to Him belongs all dominion and praise, and He is over all things omnipotent.
